What puts a graft at risk

Small gaps, serious consequences

  1. Missed or late testsRejection and infection can start before you feel anything.
  2. Medicine slipsToo little risks rejection. Too much risks toxicity.
  3. Follow-up that fadesLong journeys and busy clinics make visits easy to postpone.
  • Lower costsMedicines and lab tests at a subsidized cost.
  • Less worryTests, medicines and doctor calls are arranged for you, so nothing slips.
  • Fewer hospital tripsBlood samples are collected at home and reports reach you online.
  • Earlier warningA blood test can show graft injury before you feel unwell.
  • Someone to callA care coordinator and a nutritionist who know your history.
  • Your doctor stays in chargeEvery medical decision remains with your own transplant team.

Questions

What people ask us

Are medicines and lab tests subsidized?

Yes. Members get transplant medicines and lab tests at a subsidized cost. Your Relationship Manager will explain the prices for your plan.

Does Transplant 360 replace my transplant doctor?

No. It supports your doctor with better monitoring and follow-up. Every medical decision stays with your treating physician.

My surgery was in another city. Can I join?

Yes. You can enrol wherever your transplant was done. Care is coordinated digitally and delivered at home where possible.

Is it available outside big cities?

Yes. It covers India, including tier-2 and tier-3 cities, with home sample collection, medicine delivery and online consultations.

Which organs are covered?

Kidney, liver, heart and other solid-organ transplants. Your plan depends on the organ you received.

What is the TRIOMPH community?

TRIOMPH is our online community for transplant recipients and families, where you can share experiences and learn from others.

Is health insurance included?

Health insurance support is coming soon. Message us on WhatsApp and we will let you know when it is available.
